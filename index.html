HOW IT WORKS

From text to prediction

  1. InputA user enters a message.
  2. Pre-processingThe text is cleaned and split into words.
  3. Feature extractionCountVectorizer converts words into numerical features.
  4. ClassificationA trained Decision Tree predicts one of three categories.
Trained Decision Tree model
UNDERSTANDING THE OUTPUT

Three possible categories

The model assigns the text to one of the categories it learned from its labelled training data.

CLASS 0

Hate speech

Language that attacks or promotes prejudice against people based on identity or group membership.

CLASS 1

Offensive language

Insulting, abusive, or vulgar language that may not necessarily target a group.

CLASS 2

Neither

Text the model does not classify as hate speech or offensive language.

THINK BEFORE YOU POST

Technology helps, but people matter.

Online communication should be respectful. Pause before posting, consider how your words may affect others, and report harmful content through the appropriate platform tools when necessary.

Responsible AI notice

This project is an educational prototype. Predictions may be incorrect and can reflect limitations or bias in the training data. It should not be used as the sole basis for reporting, punishing, or silencing anyone.
